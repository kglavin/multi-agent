# Varqui Technik

Varqui Technik was founded in 2014 and makes fasteners for industrial customers. It employs 2,131 people.

The minimum order value is EUR 2,750.

It is working towards ISO 27001, expected next year, but does not hold it yet.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

The company moved its headquarters from Wroclaw, Poland, to Stuttgart, Germany, in 2022.

Standard lead time was 16 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 42 days.

Varqui Technik holds no ISO certification.

The account contact is Ines Brandt, who took over from Mara Rossi.

Quality complaints are handled by a dedicated customer desk.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.
