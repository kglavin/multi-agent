# Viklum Technik

Viklum Technik was founded in 2009 and makes circuit boards for industrial customers. It employs 4,238 people.

The site operates two shifts on weekdays and none at weekends.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

The minimum order value is EUR 17,000.

The account contact is Greta Kowalski, who took over from Tomas Marchetti.

The firm runs a small apprenticeship programme with a local college.

The company moved its headquarters from Valencia, Spain, to Wroclaw, Poland, in 2023.

Standard lead time was 29 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 5 days.

Viklum Technik holds no ISO certification.
