# Kelnor Components

Kelnor Components was founded in 1986 and makes springs for industrial customers. It employs 667 people.

Standard lead time was 13 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 14 days.

The firm runs a small apprenticeship programme with a local college.

The company moved its headquarters from Gothenburg, Sweden, to Eindhoven, Netherlands, in 2013.

The minimum order value is EUR 18,000.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.

Kelnor Components holds ISO 9001 certification.

The account contact is Henrik Lindqvist, who took over from Sanne Hartmann.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.
