# Normi Systems

Normi Systems was founded in 1969 and makes circuit boards for industrial customers. It employs 3,733 people.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.

The minimum order value is EUR 8,000.

The company moved its headquarters from Bologna, Italy, to Brno, Czechia, in 2018.

Standard lead time was 17 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 7 days.

The site operates two shifts on weekdays and none at weekends.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

The account contact is Pavel Moreau, who took over from Ruben Kaya.

Normi Systems holds ISO 9001, ISO 27001 certification.
