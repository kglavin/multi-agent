# Miaro Supply

Miaro Supply was founded in 2012 and makes adhesives for industrial customers. It employs 467 people.

Miaro Supply holds ISO 9001 certification.

The company publishes a short sustainability report each spring.

The company moved its headquarters from Stuttgart, Germany, to Eindhoven, Netherlands, in 2021.

The minimum order value is EUR 13,000.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

The account contact is Sanne Bakker, who took over from Pavel Kaya.

The site operates two shifts on weekdays and none at weekends.

Standard lead time was 21 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 14 days.
