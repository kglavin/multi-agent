# Lummi Systems

Lummi Systems was founded in 2008 and makes bearings for industrial customers. It employs 370 people.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

The company moved its headquarters from Wroclaw, Poland, to Eindhoven, Netherlands, in 2015.

The minimum order value is EUR 4,750.

Standard lead time was 39 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 27 days.

The account contact is Pavel Ferreira, who took over from Noor Duarte.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.

Quality complaints are handled by a dedicated customer desk.

Lummi Systems holds ISO 27001 certification.
