# Selaro Components

Selaro Components was founded in 1974 and makes bearings for industrial customers. It employs 4,073 people.

The minimum order value is EUR 12,750.

Standard lead time was 11 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 27 days.

The company publishes a short sustainability report each spring.

Selaro Components holds ISO 9001, ISO 27001 certification.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.

The account contact is Ada Holm, who took over from Mara Duarte.

Quality complaints are handled by a dedicated customer desk.

The company moved its headquarters from Lyon, France, to Eindhoven, Netherlands, in 2020.
