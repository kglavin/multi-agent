# Norsel Works

Norsel Works was founded in 1995 and makes cable harnesses for industrial customers. It employs 3,261 people.

The company moved its headquarters from Turin, Italy, to Wroclaw, Poland, in 2006.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

Standard lead time was 10 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 28 days.

Norsel Works holds ISO 9001 certification.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.

The account contact is Mara Marchetti, who took over from Ada Bakker.

The minimum order value is EUR 16,000.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.
