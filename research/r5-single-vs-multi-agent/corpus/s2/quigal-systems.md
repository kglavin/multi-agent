# Quigal Systems

Quigal Systems was founded in 1993 and makes bearings for industrial customers. It employs 1,776 people.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.

The company moved its headquarters from Eindhoven, Netherlands, to Brno, Czechia, in 2014.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

It is working towards ISO 27001, expected next year, but does not hold it yet.

Quigal Systems holds ISO 14001 certification.

Standard lead time was 15 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 30 days.

The account contact is Katja Moreau, who took over from Noor Visser.

The company publishes a short sustainability report each spring.

The minimum order value is EUR 14,250.
