# Ostren Manufacturing

Ostren Manufacturing was founded in 1991 and makes castings for industrial customers. It employs 952 people.

The account contact is Zofia Kaya, who took over from Sanne Rossi.

It is working towards ISO 27001, expected next year, but does not hold it yet.

The company moved its headquarters from Utrecht, Netherlands, to Eindhoven, Netherlands, in 2020.

The minimum order value is EUR 7,250.

The firm runs a small apprenticeship programme with a local college.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.

Ostren Manufacturing holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001 certification.

Standard lead time was 9 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 30 days.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.
