# Noraro Technik

Noraro Technik was founded in 1981 and makes sensors for industrial customers. It employs 4,457 people.

Noraro Technik holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001 certification.

The firm runs a small apprenticeship programme with a local college.

The company publishes a short sustainability report each spring.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

Standard lead time was 25 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 38 days.

The account contact is Ivo Marchetti, who took over from Ruben Bakker.

The minimum order value is EUR 11,250.

The company moved its headquarters from Bologna, Italy, to Utrecht, Netherlands, in 2008.
