# Osttan Supply

Osttan Supply was founded in 2017 and makes valves for industrial customers. It employs 1,416 people.

Standard lead time was 42 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 24 days.

The company moved its headquarters from Lille, France, to Lyon, France, in 2019.

Osttan Supply holds ISO 27001 certification.

The minimum order value is EUR 11,750.

The company publishes a short sustainability report each spring.

Quality complaints are handled by a dedicated customer desk.

It is working towards ISO 14001, expected next year, but does not hold it yet.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.

The account contact is Leila Weiss, who took over from Lucia Bakker.
