# Galost Components

Galost Components was founded in 1996 and makes springs for industrial customers. It employs 4,783 people.

The company moved its headquarters from Stuttgart, Germany, to Utrecht, Netherlands, in 2005.

The company publishes a short sustainability report each spring.

The minimum order value is EUR 3,250.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.

The account contact is Henrik Visser, who took over from Henrik Bakker.

Galost Components holds ISO 14001, ISO 27001 certification.

It keeps a modest stock of spare parts for older product lines.

It is working towards ISO 9001, expected next year, but does not hold it yet.

Standard lead time was 42 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 25 days.
