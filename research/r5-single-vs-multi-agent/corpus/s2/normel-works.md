# Normel Works

Normel Works was founded in 1993 and makes sensors for industrial customers. It employs 239 people.

The site operates two shifts on weekdays and none at weekends.

Normel Works holds ISO 9001, ISO 27001 certification.

The account contact is Mara Okafor, who took over from Ivo Novak.

Standard lead time was 34 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 39 days.

The company moved its headquarters from Gdansk, Poland, to Wroclaw, Poland, in 2016.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.

Quality complaints are handled by a dedicated customer desk.

The minimum order value is EUR 18,750.
