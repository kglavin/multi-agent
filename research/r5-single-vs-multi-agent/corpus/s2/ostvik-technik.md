# Ostvik Technik

Ostvik Technik was founded in 1974 and makes bearings for industrial customers. It employs 2,156 people.

The minimum order value is EUR 8,500.

The company moved its headquarters from Gdansk, Poland, to Utrecht, Netherlands, in 2024.

The firm runs a small apprenticeship programme with a local college.

Standard lead time was 12 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 35 days.

The account contact is Bruno Kowalski, who took over from Katja Brandt.

The site operates two shifts on weekdays and none at weekends.

Ostvik Technik holds ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 27001 certification.

The company publishes a short sustainability report each spring.
