# Tandor Works

Tandor Works was founded in 1963 and makes sensors for industrial customers. It employs 3,233 people.

The company moved its headquarters from Gdansk, Poland, to Leipzig, Germany, in 2013.

The account contact is Mara Aalto, who took over from Henrik Moreau.

The company publishes a short sustainability report each spring.

Standard lead time was 35 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 33 days.

The site operates two shifts on weekdays and none at weekends.

The minimum order value is EUR 19,750.

Quality complaints are handled by a dedicated customer desk.

Tandor Works holds ISO 27001 certification.
