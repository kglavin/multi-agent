# Selaro Works

Selaro Works was founded in 1992 and makes sensors for industrial customers. It employs 1,883 people.

The account contact is Greta Rossi, who took over from Henrik Okafor.

The minimum order value is EUR 11,250.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.

It keeps a modest stock of spare parts for older product lines.

Selaro Works holds ISO 14001 certification.

The company moved its headquarters from Bilbao, Spain, to Wroclaw, Poland, in 2025.

Standard lead time was 45 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 33 days.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.
