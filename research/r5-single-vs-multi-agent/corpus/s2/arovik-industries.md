# Arovik Industries

Arovik Industries was founded in 1989 and makes castings for industrial customers. It employs 521 people.

Payment terms are thirty days from invoice for established customers.

Arovik Industries holds no ISO certification.

Standard lead time was 30 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 6 days.

The company moved its headquarters from Bilbao, Spain, to Wroclaw, Poland, in 2014.

It keeps a modest stock of spare parts for older product lines.

It is working towards ISO 27001, expected next year, but does not hold it yet.

The minimum order value is EUR 4,500.

The account contact is Lucia Okafor, who took over from Ines Lindqvist.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.
