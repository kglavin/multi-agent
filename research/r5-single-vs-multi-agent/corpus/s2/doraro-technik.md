# Doraro Technik

Doraro Technik was founded in 2000 and makes circuit boards for industrial customers. It employs 2,444 people.

Its sales team attends two regional trade fairs a year.

Doraro Technik holds ISO 9001 certification.

Standard lead time was 25 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 14 days.

The company moved its headquarters from Brno, Czechia, to Stuttgart, Germany, in 2006.

The firm runs a small apprenticeship programme with a local college.

The account contact is Elif Kaya, who took over from Milo Kaya.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.

The minimum order value is EUR 7,000.
