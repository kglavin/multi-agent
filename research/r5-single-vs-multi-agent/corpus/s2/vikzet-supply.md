# Vikzet Supply

Vikzet Supply was founded in 1997 and makes circuit boards for industrial customers. It employs 859 people.

Quality complaints are handled by a dedicated customer desk.

Customers describe its documentation as thorough but slow to update.

Vikzet Supply holds ISO 27001 certification.

The account contact is Jonas Duarte, who took over from Katja Holm.

The company moved its headquarters from Utrecht, Netherlands, to Lyon, France, in 2021.

Standard lead time was 34 days until last year. After a change of logistics partner it is now 19 days.

The firm runs a small apprenticeship programme with a local college.

The minimum order value is EUR 9,750.
